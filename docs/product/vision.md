# Product vision

Services is a neutral marketplace and professional network for clients, independent specialists and organizations.

## Value proposition
A customer can find a particular professional even after they change workplaces, or discover which professionals offer a service at a business. A person may pursue multiple professions. A business maintains its own identity, locations and reputation despite staff turnover.

## Actors
- Person/account holder: may act as client, professional or owner/operator of one or more companies.
- Professional profile: one per canonical profession per person, independent public page, offers, portfolio and reviews.
- Organization: business identity, branches, team, services and reputation.
- Client: search, follow, request services, leave experience-bound feedback.
- Platform operator: moderation and verification.

## Differentiation
Bidirectional confirmed affiliations, independent professional reputation, profession-specific page composition, separately attributable service delivery and business responsibility, plus consistent profile lifecycle.

## MVP strategy
Launch one region and one vertical (e.g. hair services). Keep the underlying taxonomy and links general. Validate repeated visits, follows, completed bookings, and adoption by businesses.

## Languages and channels
Kazakh and Russian first; English-ready. Web with indexable public profiles, PWA, native Android/iOS via Capacitor.

## Business model hypotheses
Free core profiles; paid professional and business productivity tools, transparently marked sponsored placements, optional transaction attribution. Paid status must not purchase higher organic ratings or deletion of legitimate criticism.

## Trust and legal
Avoid exposing personal or medical data in public reviews. Verify regulated credentials where applicable. Validate Kazakhstan privacy, storage, consumer, advertising, tax and healthcare requirements before launch.
